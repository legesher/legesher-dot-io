Legesher is a software localization company working to build the bridge between the languages of the world and technology of the digital future.

This Cookie Policy explains how and when Legesher and our service providers use cookies, scripts and similar technologies (“**Cookies**”) when you use our Services. For more information about cookies, see [https://www.aboutcookies.org/cookie-faq](https://www.aboutcookies.org/cookie-faq). Terms used in this policy but not defined here have their meaning set forth in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy), which also includes additional details about the collection and use of information at Legesher.

## 1. How Legesher Uses Cookies {#how-we-use-cookies}

We use cookies to manage the Services and to collect information about you and your use of the Services. Cookies help us to recognize you, customize or personalize your experience, and analyze the use of our Services to make them more useful to you.

| Purpose | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Necessary | These are cookies necessary to use our Services, for example: cookies to help us detect and prevent malicious activity or fraudulent usage, and when you sign in to use our Services, cookies that help us show you the correct information and preferences associated with your account. |
| Analytics | We use cookieless analytics, which set no cookies and store nothing in your browser, to understand the general usage of, and to improve, our Services (for example, to help us measure how many people interact with our Services). We may collect Technical Information, such as pages visited and how your web browser interacts with our Services. |
| Marketing | We do not use Cookies for marketing. |

## 2. Managing Your Cookie Settings {#managing-cookies}

Most browsers allow you to remove or manage cookie functions and adjust your privacy and security preferences. For information on how to do this, access the “help” menu on your browser, or access [https://www.aboutcookies.org/how-to-control-cookies](https://www.aboutcookies.org/how-to-control-cookies). Due to differences between websites and other technologies, you may need to take additional steps to opt out. Please check your settings for additional information. Please note, however, that disabling all cookies may mean that you may not be able to take full advantage of our Services.

You can control how and when your personal data is shared or used in the Privacy Settings of your Legesher account, or by enabling global privacy controls in your browser. Legesher does not share your personal data for advertising.

**Do Not Track**. Some web browsers provide a “Do Not Track” option. When your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, our analytics records nothing and the account app sends no usage events.

## 3. Changes to Our Cookie Policy {#changes}

Legesher may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes to this Cookie Policy, as appropriate, and update the Effective Date at the top of this Cookie Policy.
