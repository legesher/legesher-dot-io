Legesher is a software localization company working to build the bridge between the languages of the world and technology of the digital future.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and process your personal data when you use our website or other Legesher products and services (the “Services”). This Privacy Policy does not apply to content that we process on behalf of customers of our business offerings, such as our Enterprise accounts. Our use of that data is governed by our customer agreements covering access to and use of those offerings.

This Privacy Policy also describes your privacy rights. More information about your rights, and how to exercise them, is set out in Section 4 (“Rights and Choices”).

## 1. Collection of Personal Data {#information-we-collect}

### Personal Data You Provide to Us Directly {#personal-data-you-provide-to-us-directly}

| Data | What it includes |
| --- | --- |
| Account | Your email address, first name, sign-in details and GitHub link, held by our sign-in provider. Our own record holds an account id, your GitHub user id and username, your home region, the languages you choose and any founding language, the Terms version you accepted and any newsletter consent. Never a password or API key |
| Payments | Polar collects your name, email, billing address and payment details. We receive your email, plan and its status, never your card details |
| Code you send | See below |
| Newsletter | Your first name and email address, the campaign link you arrived from, and your IP address, which our newsletter provider uses for spam prevention |
| Surveys and email | What you choose to send us |
| Problem reports | What you write in the Report a problem form, plus your account id, plan, app version and browser type. We send you an acknowledgement email |

**Translation API**. Your code is processed and returned. We do not log it.

**Names kept for consistency**. In enhanced mode, so a name is translated the same way each time, we hold the names from a repository you identify, with their translations, in memory for your account only. They are never stored and are cleared when the service restarts.

**Enhanced mode**. An AI system included in paid plans that list it. It sends the comments, docstrings, text strings and names from your code, never its structure, to our AI provider in the United States, and returns suggested translations. Its only purpose is translation, set by us, and it makes no decisions about you. The provider does not train on the content and deletes it within 30 days. We keep it only as described under Translation API. We follow the OECD AI Principles.

**Connected repositories**. The Legesher GitHub App has read-only access to the repositories you choose. It reads their contents only to provide the features you use, and that code is handled like any code sent to the Translation API. Its access tokens are never stored. Uninstalling the App ends our access.

**On your own machine**. The command line, the language packs and the VS Code extension translate on your machine and send no code to anyone. If your code must not leave your region, use them.

**Contributed translations**. You contribute translations by pull request to `legesher/language-packs` on GitHub. The word you offer, and a record that it was endorsed, join the public Legesher language corpus on Hugging Face, published under CC BY 4.0. Your name does not appear: you appear as an opaque identifier, and we never publish the link back to you.

### Personal Data We Receive Automatically from Your Use of the Services {#personal-data-we-receive-automatically-from-your-use-of-the-services}

| Data | What it includes |
| --- | --- |
| Website visits | Server logs and cookieless analytics of how our website is used |
| Account usage events | Creating your account, creating or revoking an API key, starting checkout and confirming a plan, tied only to your account id. Starting sign-up is counted with a random id, not your account |

**Usage records**. For each request we keep a pseudonymous record: your account or key (or, without an account, a salted hash of your network address), the time, tool, languages, size and outcome. If you name a repository, we keep a salted hash of its name. The record never holds your code. A salted hash is a one-way code: no one can turn it back into the original.

**Website**. www.legesher.io sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.

**Account app**. app.legesher.io uses the strictly necessary cookies and storage below, set by our sign-in provider and by us, to sign you in and protect accounts from abuse. They hold no advertising or tracking data.

| Name | Type | Purpose | Expires |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `__session`, `__session_WAgH8YD0` | Cookie | Proves you are signed in | Up to 1 year |
| `__client_uat`, `__client_uat_WAgH8YD0` | Cookie, for all of legesher.io | Keeps your sign-in the same across Legesher sites | Up to about 13 months |
| `clerk_active_context` | Cookie | Remembers which session is active | When you close the browser |
| `__clerk_environment` | Local storage | Stores sign-in settings | Until cleared |
| `__clerk_protect_pid`, `__clerk_protect_st:clerk.legesher.io` | Local storage | Protects sign-in from bots | Until cleared |
| `__Host-legesher_new_key` | Cookie | Shows you a new API key once | 2 minutes |
| `__Host-legesher_keys_notice` | Cookie | Shows the result of a key request once | 2 minutes |
| `__Host-legesher_device` | Cookie | Brings you back to device sign-in after you sign in | 10 minutes |

**Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control**. When your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, our analytics records nothing and the account app sends no usage events.

**Documentation site**. docs.legesher.io is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server logs (IP addresses, request times and URLs). Its cookieless Vercel Web Analytics counts page views, referrers, country or region, device type and browser in aggregate, and stores no IP addresses or personal identifiers. If you are signed in to app.legesher.io, a script on the documentation site reads the sign-in cookie in your browser only to choose whether to show a link to your account or one to create an account. It sends nothing and sets no cookie.

**VS Code extension**. The extension translates on your machine and sends no code to anyone. If you turn on its telemetry (`legesher.telemetry.enabled`), it sends usage statistics: activation events, which commands you use, which languages you translate between, language changes (what prompted them, which settings level supplied the language, and whether the change succeeded), language detection outcomes (the language code and match score only), performance metrics, and error reports without code. It never sends your code or file contents, your name, email or IP address, project names or file paths, the names you define, or literal values from your code. The telemetry library adds a pseudonymous machine id and session id to each event. VS Code itself collects telemetry separately, under Microsoft's privacy statement.

### Personal Data We Collect or Receive to Train Our Models {#personal-data-we-collect-or-receive-to-train-our-models}

**Your Inputs and Outputs**. Unless you opt out of training in your account settings, we may keep your Inputs and Outputs to improve the Services and train our models. If you opt out, your code is not kept or used for training, except Feedback you send us about it or Materials flagged for safety review ([Terms, Section 5](/terms#inputs-and-outputs)).

**VS Code extension**. If you turn on `legesher.telemetry.contributeToMLTraining` (off by default), the extension sends feedback when you change a Legesher suggestion: the correction (for example, "media" to "promedio"), the kind of code element (function, variable or class) and the kind of operation (for example, arithmetic or string manipulation). It sends no code content and no file paths. Each event carries the same pseudonymous machine id and session id.

## 4. Rights and Choices {#your-rights}

Depending on where you live and the laws that apply in your country of residence, you may enjoy certain rights regarding your personal data, as described further below. However, please be aware that these rights are limited, and that the processes by which we may need to action your requests regarding our training dataset are complex. We may also decline a request if we have a lawful reason for doing so. That said, we strive to prioritize the protection of personal data, and comply with all applicable privacy laws.

To exercise your rights, you or an authorized agent may submit a request by [contacting us](#contact). After we receive your request, we may verify it by requesting information sufficient to confirm your identity (e.g. email address, billing details). Where a third party representative submits a request on behalf of a data subject, we require evidence of authorization to act on behalf of the data subject.

We will respond to your request within the period required by the DIFC Data Protection Law. Legesher will not discriminate based on the exercising of privacy rights you may have. Set out below is a summary of the rights which you may enjoy, depending on the laws that apply in your country of residence.

- Right to know: the right to know what personal data Legesher processes about you, including the categories of personal data, the categories of sources from which it is collected, the business or commercial purposes for collection, and the categories of third parties to whom we disclose it.
- Access & data portability: the right to request a copy of the personal data Legesher processes about you, subject to certain exceptions and conditions. In certain cases and subject to applicable law, you have the right to port your information.
- Deletion: the right to request that we delete personal data collected from you when you use our Services, subject to certain exceptions.
- Correction: the right to request that we correct inaccurate personal data Legesher retains about you, subject to certain exceptions. Please note that we cannot guarantee the factual accuracy of Outputs.
- Objection: the right to object to processing of your personal data, including profiling conducted on grounds of public or legitimate interest. In places where such a right applies, we will no longer process the personal data in case of such objection unless we demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds for the processing which override your interests, rights, and freedoms, or for the establishment, exercise or defense of legal claims. If we use your information for direct marketing, you can object and opt out of future direct marketing messages using the unsubscribe link in such communications.
- Restriction: the right to restrict our processing of your personal data in certain circumstances.
- Withdrawal of consent: Where Legesher's processing of your personal data is based on consent, you have the right to withdraw your consent.
- Automated decision-making: Legesher does not engage in decision making based solely on automated processing or profiling in a manner which produces a legal effect (i.e., impacts your legal rights) or significantly affects you in a similar way (e.g., significantly affects your financial circumstances or ability to access essential goods or services).
- Sale & targeted Legesher marketing of its products and services: Legesher does not “sell” your personal data as that term is defined by applicable laws and regulations. You can opt out of sharing your personal data for targeted advertising to promote our products and services, and we will honor global privacy controls.

Legesher gives you access to a variety of tools to help you manage your data. You can access these in your Privacy Settings.

Legesher retains your personal data for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes and criteria outlined in this Privacy Policy.

When the personal data collected is no longer required by us, we and our service providers will perform the necessary procedures for destroying, deleting, erasing, or converting it into an anonymous form as permitted or required under applicable laws.

### Aggregated or De-Identified Information {#aggregated-or-de-identified-information}

We apply aggregation and de-identification techniques where appropriate as part of our data minimization practices. For example, we may process personal data in an aggregated or de-identified form to analyze and improve the effectiveness of our Services including to conduct analytics, conduct research, study user behavior, and improve our products as permitted under applicable laws. For instance:

- When you submit Feedback, we disassociate Inputs and Outputs from your account id to use them for training and improving our models.
- If our systems flag Inputs or Outputs for harmful content or for potentially violating our policies, we disassociate the content from your account id to improve our ability to detect harmful content, enforce our policies, or advance our safety research. However, we may re-identify the Inputs or Outputs to enforce our Terms of Service or Usage Policy with the responsible user if necessary.
- To improve user experience, we may analyze and aggregate general user behavior and usage data. This information does not identify individual users.

Where data is de-identified, Legesher will maintain and use this information in its de-identified form, and will not attempt to re-identify such information, except as permitted by law.

### Security Controls Relating to Our Processing of Personal Data {#data-security}

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures designed to protect personal data from loss, misuse, and unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.
