Legesher Global Tech Ltd

You trust us with your code and your details. This page says exactly what we do with them.

## At a glance {#at-a-glance}

- Legesher Global Tech Ltd, in the DIFC, is responsible for your personal data.
- We do not sell your data, and we do not use your code to train models.
- Code you send to Legesher is processed and not kept.
- You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time.

The full text below governs.

## 1. Who we are {#who-we-are}

Legesher Global Tech Ltd ("Legesher", "we") is responsible for your personal data. Our Data Protection Officer can be reached at [support@legesher.com](mailto:support@legesher.com). Our affiliate Legesher Inc does not receive your personal data.

This policy covers www.legesher.io, your account at app.legesher.io, the Translation API at api.legesher.io, and our newsletter and surveys. The VS Code extension has its own [privacy page](https://docs.legesher.io/privacy).

## 3. Code you send to Legesher {#your-code}

3.1 **Translation API.** Your code is processed in memory and returned. It is not logged, not kept and not used to train models.

3.2 **Usage records.** For each request we keep a pseudonymous record: your account or key (or, without an account, a salted hash of your network address), the time, tool, languages, size and outcome. If you name a repository, we keep a salted hash of its name. The record never holds your code. A salted hash is a one-way code: no one can turn it back into the original.

3.3 **Names kept for consistency.** In enhanced mode, so a name is translated the same way each time, we hold the names from a repository you identify, with their translations, in memory for your account only. They are never stored and are cleared when the service restarts.

3.4 **Enhanced mode.** An AI system included in paid plans that list it. It sends the comments, docstrings, text strings and names from your code, never its structure, to our AI provider in the United States, and returns suggested translations. Its only purpose is translation, set by us, and it makes no decisions about you. The provider does not train on the content and deletes it within 30 days; we keep none of it. We follow the OECD AI Principles.

3.5 **Connected repositories.** The Legesher GitHub App has read-only access to the repositories you choose. It reads their contents only to provide the features you use, and that code is handled as in 3.1. Its access tokens are never stored. Uninstalling the App ends our access.

3.6 **On your own machine.** The command line, the language packs and the VS Code extension translate on your machine and send no code to anyone. If your code must not leave your region, use them.

4.1 www.legesher.io sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser.

4.2 app.legesher.io uses the strictly necessary cookies and storage below, set by our sign-in provider and by us, to sign you in and protect accounts from abuse. They hold no advertising or tracking data.

| Name | Type | Purpose | Expires |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `__session`, `__session_WAgH8YD0` | Cookie | Proves you are signed in | Up to 1 year |
| `__client_uat`, `__client_uat_WAgH8YD0` | Cookie, for all of legesher.io | Keeps your sign-in the same across Legesher sites | Up to about 13 months |
| `clerk_active_context` | Cookie | Remembers which session is active | When you close the browser |

| `__Host-legesher_keys_notice` | Cookie | Shows the result of a key request once | 2 minutes |
| `__Host-legesher_device` | Cookie | Brings you back to device sign-in after you sign in | 10 minutes |

4.3 When your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, our analytics records nothing and the account app sends no usage events.

We do not sell your data or use it for targeted advertising. We share it only with the providers in Appendix A, who process it for us under contract; when the law requires it; as part of a merger or sale of the business; or with your consent.

## 8. Contributed translations {#community}

You contribute translations by pull request to `legesher/language-packs` on GitHub. The word you offer, and a record that it was endorsed, join the public Legesher language corpus on Hugging Face, published under CC BY 4.0. Your name does not appear: you appear as an opaque identifier, and we never publish the link back to you.

You can ask us to erase the data that identifies you. Your translations stay in the corpus, with nothing linking them to you. We cannot remove a public pull request or issue you made under your own GitHub account.

## 9. Your rights {#your-rights}

Under the DIFC Data Protection Law (DIFC Law No. 5 of 2020), you can ask to access, correct, delete, restrict or move your data, object to how we use it (including for marketing), and withdraw consent. You also have the right not to be subject to decisions made only by automated processing that significantly affect you. Some rights apply only in certain situations. Using them never affects how we treat you.

Email [support@legesher.com](mailto:support@legesher.com). You do not need an account. We respond within one month, or tell you within that month if we need up to two more.

You can delete your account in Settings. To also delete your account usage events, ask us at the same address.

You can complain to the DIFC Commissioner of Data Protection (commissioner@dp.difc.ae) or apply to the DIFC Courts.

## 10. Children {#childrens-privacy}

Accounts are for people aged 18 and over, unless a school or organization gives access under a separate agreement with us ([Terms, Section 2.1](/terms#your-account)). If we learn that an account belongs to someone younger without such an agreement, we close it and delete its data. The command line, the language packs and the VS Code extension need no account.

## 11. Security {#data-security}

We encrypt data in transit, store API keys only as hashes, limit access to our systems and review our security each year. No system is completely secure.

## 12. Changes {#changes}

When we update this policy, we update the date at the top of this policy. We tell you about material changes by email or on the website.
