This policy explains what happens when you cancel a paid Legesher subscription, when you can get a refund, and what happens if a payment fails. It applies to every paid plan sold through our checkout. It forms part of our [Terms of Service](/terms). On cancellations and refunds this policy controls; on everything else the Terms do.

Nothing in this policy affects the Legesher open-source software, which is free under the Apache 2.0 License and is not a subscription. You never need a paid plan to write code in your language with the open-source tools.

<p><span data-review="jurisdiction">If you are a consumer, you may have rights under the law of your country that this policy cannot reduce. Where that law gives you more than this policy does, the law applies.</span></p>

## 1. Who Bills You {#who-bills-you}

Paid Legesher subscriptions are sold through <a href="https://polar.sh" rel="noopener">Polar</a>, which acts as the merchant of record. <span data-review="jurisdiction">Polar is the seller on your receipt, collects payment and any applicable sales tax or VAT, and issues refunds; Legesher Inc decides the plan, the price and this policy.</span> Polar's own terms apply to the payment itself.

<p><span data-review="processor">Your subscription, invoices, payment method and cancellation are all managed from the Polar customer portal. The link to the portal is in every receipt email.</span></p>

## 2. Cancelling a Subscription {#cancellation}

<p><span data-review="processor">You can cancel at any time from the Polar customer portal.</span> You do not need to email or call us.</p>

- **Access continues to the end of the period you paid for.** Cancelling stops the next renewal; it does not end the current period early. Your plan's features keep working until the paid period ends, then your account moves to the Free plan.
- <span data-review="processor">**You can undo a cancellation** from the portal at any time before the period ends, and the subscription continues as if nothing happened.</span>
- **Nothing you built is removed.** Code and translations in your own repositories stay there. When the paid period ends, the features of your paid plan stop and your account continues on the Free plan. Library names are shown in their original form again; your own names keep their translations. Your account is kept until you ask us to delete it (see Section 6).
- **Offers.** An offer you received at checkout applies for the term stated there. If you cancel and subscribe again later, you join at the price in force at that time.

## 3. Refunds {#refunds}

### 3.1 New subscriptions

<p><span data-review="jurisdiction">If a paid plan is not what you expected, ask for a refund within **14 days** of your first payment and we will refund that payment in full.</span> For a yearly plan, Section 3.3 says when the 14 days start. After that window, the first period is not refundable, but you can cancel at any time (Section 2).</p>

<p><span data-review="jurisdiction">The 14-day window is our policy and applies worldwide. If the consumer law where you live gives you a longer withdrawal period for digital services, that period applies instead.</span></p>

### 3.2 Renewals

<p><span data-review="jurisdiction">A renewal charged in error, or charged after you tried to cancel and the cancellation did not take effect, is refunded in full if you contact us within 14 days of the charge.</span> Otherwise renewals are not refunded; cancel before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.</p>

### 3.3 Yearly plans

A yearly plan is paid up front. <span data-review="jurisdiction">The 14-day refund in 3.1 applies to your first yearly payment, counted from the later of the payment and the day the plan's features are available to you.</span> After that the year is not refunded, and cancelling stops the next renewal. We email you a reminder before a yearly plan renews.

### 3.4 Enterprise agreements

Enterprise customers are billed by invoice under a signed agreement. Refunds and termination for those agreements follow the agreement, not this policy.

### 3.5 How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the original payment method through Polar. <span data-review="processor">Once approved, a refund usually appears within 5 to 10 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer.</span> When we refund a payment, the paid plan it bought ends.

### 3.6 What is not refunded

- Usage already consumed, except inside the windows above
- Charges for a period in which the account was suspended for a breach of the Terms
- Currency conversion fees or bank charges applied by your own bank

## 4. Failed Payments {#failed-payments}

<p><span data-review="processor">If a renewal payment fails, we tell you by email and Polar retries the payment.</span> During the retry period your account runs with Free plan limits and nothing is removed.</p>

- **Restoring your plan.** <span data-review="processor">Polar retries the payment over the 21 days after the first failed charge. Saving a new default payment method in the Polar portal retries it at once.</span> As soon as a payment succeeds, your paid plan is restored.
- **When the retries end.** <span data-review="processor">If no payment has succeeded when Polar stops retrying, your subscription ends and we email you to say so.</span> Your account stays on the Free plan, and you can subscribe again at any time.
- **Non-payment deletes nothing.** <span data-review="jurisdiction">Your API keys keep working with Free plan limits, and your usage records stay linked to your account. Your account is kept until you ask us to delete it (Section 6).</span> Data in your own repositories is never touched.

## 5. Upgrades, Downgrades and Price Changes {#plan-changes}

- **Upgrades** take effect immediately. <span data-review="processor">Polar charges the prorated difference for the rest of the current period.</span>
- <span data-review="processor">**Downgrades** take effect at the end of the current period.</span> You keep what you paid for until then; no partial refund is issued for the difference.
- **Price changes.** The price you were quoted at checkout is the price you pay for that period.

## 6. Your Data After Cancellation {#data-after-cancellation}

Cancelling a subscription is not the same as deleting your account. Your account is kept after your subscription ends, until you ask us to delete it, so you can return without starting over. To delete your account and its hosted data, use Delete account on the Settings page of your Legesher account once your subscription has ended, or email [privacy@legesher.com](mailto:privacy@legesher.com); how we handle that request is described in our [Privacy Policy](/privacy).

## 7. How to Ask for a Refund {#how-to-ask}

1. Email [hello@legesher.com](mailto:hello@legesher.com) from the address on your account.
2. Include the receipt number from Polar (it is on the receipt email) and the reason, if you want to share one.
3. We reply within <span data-review="jurisdiction">3 business days</span>. <span data-review="processor">Approved refunds are issued through Polar straight away.</span>

Disagreements about a refund are handled under the dispute section of our [Terms of Service](/terms#governing-law).

## 8. Changes to This Policy {#changes}

We may update this policy. The date at the top changes when we do, and for material changes we email subscribers before the change takes effect. A change never reduces the refund rights of a period you have already paid for.
